01

The problem

Habbly is a strong exam-prep product, but the only way in today is through a school. Everything below comes from Habbly's own codebase.

One door, and only a school can open it

A student plays Habbly by joining a class with a class code + PIN. No class means no Habbly. A parent who wants extra GCSE practice for their child has nothing to buy.

Source: Habbly's current student sign-in flow

Learning stops when homework stops

Lessons arrive as homework. Holidays, revision season and the gap after a homework is done have no pull to come back. The points students earn (50 / 45 / 40 per answer) only feed that day's score.

Source: Habbly's current scoring rules

All revenue depends on school deals

Every pound comes from a school contract, which means long sales cycles and renewals once a year. There's no direct, monthly revenue line, even though the hardest parts of a consumer app are already built.

Google, Apple and email-OTP sign-in already exist in the Habbly app
Why it matters to Jonathan's business: Habbly already has the hard parts of a consumer app: 20+ game formats, AI marking, and social sign-in. It just has no consumer front door and no reason to come back every day. Habbly Plus adds both without touching the school flow.
02

The reward loop

Chests and timed windows are proven mobile-game mechanics. Here, every reward is earned by learning: none can be bought with money.

10-minute windowA daily lesson unlocks. Finish it within 10 minutes for +300 bonus points.
Play the lessonReal Habbly formats such as BPD, scored by the real timer zones.
Earn a chestGold for finishing inside the window, Silver if you finish late. Chests unlock on a timer.
Mystery boxBeat Ana (the ghost opponent) to unlock it. The possible contents are always shown.
Come back tomorrowYour streak grows. Streak freezes and double-points boosts come from chests.

03

Why it will work

About 70% of it is already built

Social and OTP login, 20+ formats, AI marking, a ghost opponent ("Ana" in BPD), and the points system are all live. Plus adds a paywall, the reward layer and a second tab. The school flow stays as it is.

Balloon Pop Duel is a live Habbly format today

Rewards sit on scoring that already exists

The +300 bonus and chest drops are calculated from the same points the host already already records. Formats don't change, so there's no new scoring logic to get wrong.

Uses Habbly's existing timer-zone points

It gives students a reason to come back tomorrow

Schools give students a reason to start. A timed window plus a chest on a timer gives them a reason to return, which is the habit Habbly's name promises.

04

Why it might not work

RiskWhy it's realMitigation
Loot-box rules for childrenPaid random rewards for under-18s face scrutiny in the UK and EU, and parents and schools will notice.Chests and the mystery box are never sold, only earned by learning. Contents and odds are shown. No coin purchases at all.
Under-13 sign-upGoogle and Apple sign-in for children has extra consent rules (UK Children's Code, COPPA).An age gate at sign-up. Under-13s go through parent email + OTP, and the parent pays.
Plus could cannibalise school salesA school could tell students to "just buy Plus" instead of paying for a licence.Plus has no teacher dashboard and no homework setting, so schools still need their licence. Students at a Habbly school could get Plus at a discount, which makes schools the sales channel.
Rewards can crowd out learningStudents may chase chests and rush answers.Chests depend on finishing the lesson, not on speed alone. The timer zones (50 / 45 / 40) still reward accuracy first: a wrong answer earns 0.
App-store fees and design sign-offApple and Google take 15–30% of subscriptions. Jonathan owns Habbly's design, so a dark "Plus" theme needs his approval.Offer web checkout for parents where allowed. Agree the Plus visual language with Jonathan before any build.
05

Business potential

For Habbly: a second revenue line

  • Monthly subscription paid by parents: proposed £5.99/month or £39.99/year with a 7-day free trial (to be tested).
  • Revenue all year, including holidays and revision season.
  • Students who leave a Habbly school can keep using it.

For Habbly: better school numbers too

  • The streak and window bring students back on days without homework.
  • More practice means better results, which is the strongest case at school renewal time.
  • "Your students can also use Plus at home" becomes a selling point.

For Codewave

  • Build: the Plus tab, paywall, reward engine and chest UI (the logins already exist).
  • Ongoing: a monthly retainer for seasonal events, new chests and A/B tests on price and rewards.
  • Reuse: a learning-rewards engine we can offer other edtech clients.

What we'd measure in the first month (no guesses here, these get measured)

Trial start rate from the Plus tab · trial → paid conversion · share of lessons finished inside the 10-minute window · day-7 return rate (Plus vs School-only students) · chest opens per active student.

How we found it

Jonathan has already discussed a premium tier with us and wants it live as soon as possible. Building Habbly's app with Jonathan, we saw that every login path except class code + PIN already exists in the code, and that formats like Balloon Pop Duel already feel like a game. The one thing missing is a way for families to pay and a reason to come back daily.

Next steps: launch as soon as possible

Premium is already on Jonathan's roadmap, and Jonathan wants it soon. This prototype is the starting point for that plan.

  • Week 1: agree the Plus design and reward table with Jonathan.
  • Weeks 2–3: build the paywall, reward engine and chest UI on the existing app and its sign-in.
  • Week 4: soft-launch to families at Habbly schools, then measure trial → paid.
